## Description

Bump is where you find the café worth crossing town for — from the people who already went.

Every post is one place: the photos, a rating out of five, and where to find it. Tap the
shop and you see every photo anyone has posted of it, so you know what you're walking into
before you go.

Follow the people whose taste you trust and your feed turns into a short list of places
worth trying. Share your own in about thirty seconds — snap it, rate it, say where.

**WHAT YOU CAN DO**

• Post a place in seconds — up to six square photos, a rating, a caption, and the shop's
name and address
• Open Search and it's already a list of every shop on Bump — browse first, type only when
you're after something specific
• See a shop's rating, address and every photo posted about it, with directions one tap away
• Follow people, like posts, and ask the question you actually have in the comments
• Save the places you want to remember, and keep your own in a grid on your profile
• Turn on location — entirely optional — for the top-rated shops within five kilometres
• Get told when someone likes your post, comments on it, or follows you

**BUILT TO BE PLEASANT**

• Light and dark, warm rather than clinical, following your system setting or your choice
• Scales with Dynamic Type and works with VoiceOver
• English and Tiếng Việt
• Block or report anyone, at any time

Bump is for the places you'd tell a friend about. Post one, and someone will.

## Notes for the submission form

- **Category:** Food & Drink (primary). Social Networking is the sensible secondary.
- **Age rating:** the app carries user-generated photos, captions and comments, so answer
  the UGC questions honestly — blocking and reporting are both implemented, which is what
  App Review looks for alongside the answer.
- **Privacy:** location is optional and only used for nearby shops and for pinning a post's
  shop; photos come from the camera or the library at the moment of posting. Declare both.
- **Support URL and privacy policy URL are required** and are not part of the build.
